# Sales Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing ride-booking and customer behavior data using Python and Power BI. The objective of the project is to transform raw booking data into meaningful business insights that can help improve customer experience, operational efficiency, revenue growth, and cancellation management.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow including:

* Data cleaning and preprocessing using Python
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Data visualization and dashboard creation in Power BI
* Business insights generation
* Actionable business recommendations

---

# Problem Statement

Ride-booking companies generate large volumes of booking and customer data every day. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to:

* Understand customer booking patterns
* Identify revenue-driving factors
* Track cancellation trends
* Analyze vehicle performance
* Improve customer satisfaction
* Optimize operational efficiency

This project solves these challenges by building an interactive Power BI dashboard supported by Python-based data preparation and analysis.

# Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to visualize booking trends, customer behavior, revenue performance, vehicle analysis, ratings, and cancellations.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Booking Status Breakdown

This section provides an overview of booking distribution and operational performance.

#### Key Insights

* Total Booking Value: 100K
* Successful Bookings: 62%
* Average Booking Value: 31.63K
* Booking statuses include:

  * Success
  * Cancelled by Customer
  * Cancelled by Driver
  * Incomplete
  * No Driver Found

#### Business Insight

A majority of bookings are successful, indicating stable operational performance. However, cancellation categories still represent opportunities for improvement.

---

### 2. Revenue Dashboard

This dashboard focuses on revenue generation patterns.

#### Analysis Included

* Revenue by payment method
* Monthly revenue distribution
* Top customers by booking value
* Revenue contribution analysis

#### Key Findings

* UPI contributes nearly 40% of total revenue.
* Certain customers generate significantly higher booking value.
* Revenue fluctuates across different periods.

#### Business Insight

Digital payment methods are highly preferred and should be promoted further through cashback offers and loyalty rewards.

---

### 3. Vehicle Type Performance

This dashboard compares the performance of different vehicle categories.

#### Metrics Analyzed

* Total Bookings
* Success Rate
* Average Distance
* Total Revenue

#### Key Findings

* Prime SUV generated the highest booking value.
* Some vehicle types show lower operational efficiency.
* Revenue varies significantly across vehicle categories.

#### Business Insight

High-performing vehicle categories should receive greater operational focus and marketing promotion.

---

### 4. Cancellation Deep Dive

This section analyzes cancellation behavior.

#### Cancellation Categories

* Cancelled by Customer
* Cancelled by Driver

#### Common Reasons

* Driver unavailable
* Long waiting time
* Customer changed plans
* Payment-related issues
* Traffic and operational delays

#### Business Insight

Reducing waiting time and improving driver allocation can help lower cancellation rates.

---

### 5. Ratings Overview

This dashboard evaluates customer and driver ratings.

#### Analysis Included

* Driver ratings by vehicle type
* Customer ratings by vehicle type
* Overall rating comparison

#### Key Findings

* Most vehicle types maintain ratings above 4.5.
* Premium vehicle categories tend to receive better ratings.
* Customer satisfaction remains generally positive.

#### Business Insight

Maintaining service quality and driver professionalism is essential for customer retention.

---

# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

EDA helped uncover hidden patterns and trends in the dataset.

## Major Observations

* Successful bookings dominate overall transactions.
* UPI is the most preferred payment method.
* Prime SUV performs best in terms of booking value.
* Cancellation rates are influenced by operational delays.
* High customer ratings indicate strong user satisfaction.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

## 1. Reduce Cancellations

* Improve driver allocation efficiency.
* Reduce customer waiting time.
* Introduce real-time booking updates.

## 2. Improve Customer Satisfaction

* Maintain service quality standards.
* Encourage driver professionalism.
* Reward highly rated drivers.

## 3. Target High-Demand Zones

* Increase vehicle availability in busy areas.
* Use demand forecasting for better planning.

## 4. Strengthen Digital Payments

* Promote UPI and online payment offers.
* Introduce cashback and loyalty rewards.

## 5. Optimize Vehicle Allocation

* Focus on high-performing vehicle categories.
* Improve operational management for underperforming segments.
